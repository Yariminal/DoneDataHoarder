## Capacity qualification

The full combined proof hashed 4,431,413,374 physical bytes. The 100,000-row and nominal 524 GB fixtures were logical/metadata tests without equivalent physical bytes. Current drivers cap a staged archive at 4 GiB and combined extraction at 12 GiB/20,000 files. A post-run observation on 2026-09-28 found 138,132,848,640 bytes free on the data volume and 455,612,387,328 bytes on the system volume; available space can change. The data volume cannot stage a 100 GB source plus a full disposable copy with safe headroom, and these volumes cannot stage a 500 GB source plus copy. Recheck capacity before scale testing.

The next scale gate needs provisioned storage or a separately reviewed streaming harness, then a **physically backed 100 GB** collection with complete manifest/hash reconciliation, wall throughput, temporary/WAL/undo headroom, and Python/Ollama/GPU peaks. Dense perceptual/semantic candidate groups must report examined and deferred denominators, bounded-stage time and memory, and keeper-relative review quality; capped search must never be reported as no duplicates. On disposable linked-project trees, measure preview/commit latency and inject executor interruptions around filesystem/DB/journal transitions, verifying byte, path, topology, reference, and undo recovery. Escalate to 500 GB only after those gates pass. The current evidence does not establish 100–500 GB readiness.
